Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Hermes is a personal assistant used only by its owner, Vaibhav Nayel, with his own Google accounts. No other users are served.

What Google data Hermes accesses

When the owner authorises it, Hermes can access Gmail messages and labels, Google Calendar events, Google Drive files, Google Docs, Google Sheets and Google Contacts in the owner's accounts.

How the data is used

The data is used only to carry out tasks the owner requests, such as summarising email, preparing a daily brief, scheduling events or editing documents. It is not used for advertising, not sold, and not used to train general-purpose AI models.

Storage and sharing

OAuth tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer. Content is fetched on demand and may be sent to the AI model provider the owner has configured, solely to complete the requested task. Data is not shared with any other third party.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Hermes' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
