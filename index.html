Hermes

Hermes is a private, single-user AI assistant that Vaibhav Nayel runs on his own computer. It is not a public product and does not accept sign-ups.

With the owner's permission, Hermes connects to the owner's own Google accounts (Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Google Docs, Google Sheets and Google Contacts) so it can read, summarise and, when the owner asks, draft or send email, manage calendar events and work with documents on his behalf.

See the Privacy Policy for how Google user data is handled, and the Terms of Service.
